    HFC Basket filters

    Manual filters for all applications, with a large filtering surface. Available as simplex, duplex (HFCD), vertical, horizontal, in-line and other configurations. Built in carbon steel, stainless steel, duplex and super duplex, Monel and more.

    Flow manual

    Filtration 1 µm to several mm

    Advantages

    • Filtration grades from 1 micron to several millimetres.
    • Large filtering surface.
    • Options: simplex, duplex, vertical, horizontal, in-line.
    • Heavy construction for long service life and very low maintenance.
